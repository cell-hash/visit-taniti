Dining and groceries

Ten restaurants on the island: five serving mostly local fish and rice, three American style, and two Pan-Asian.

Harbor Table

$$

Local fish and rice · Taniti City

Fish landed the same morning, served with rice. The city's best-known dining room.

Yellow Leaf Fish House

$$

Local fish and rice · Yellow Leaf Bay

Grilled local fish and rice on a terrace above the bay.

Rice and Reef

$

Local fish and rice · Taniti City

A small family kitchen serving fish and rice at lunch and dinner.

Volcano Road Kitchen

$

Local fish and rice · Interior

A roadside dining room on the way to the volcano, popular with hiking groups.

Nane's Fish and Rice

$

Local fish and rice · Merriton Landing

A counter-service local kitchen open late on the north side of the bay.

The Landing Grill

$$

American style · Merriton Landing

American-style burgers, steaks, and salads, with a children's menu.

Bayside Diner

$

American style · Taniti City

Breakfast all day, American style, near the bus stops on Harbor Road.

Merriton Brewhouse

$$

American style · Merriton Landing

The island's microbrewery, with American pub food. Alcohol is not served between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Pacific Spice

$$

Pan-Asian · Taniti City

Pan-Asian plates for sharing, in the native-architecture quarter.

Rainforest Noodle Bar

$

Pan-Asian · Merriton Landing

Noodles and dumplings, quick service, open for lunch and dinner.

Groceries and supplies

Two supermarkets

Full-size supermarkets in Taniti City, useful if your lodging has a kitchen.

Two grocery stores

Smaller neighborhood grocery stores for daily items.

One 24-hour convenience store

Open around the clock, although alcohol cannot be sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.
