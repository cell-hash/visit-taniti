Travel essentials

The questions visitors ask most often, answered in one place. Select a question to read the answer.

The drinking age on Taniti is 18. Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m., including at the microbrewery and the 24-hour convenience store.

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Several banks handle currency exchange, and major credit cards are widely accepted.

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States, so U.S. plugs and chargers work without an adapter.

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English, which covers most of Taniti City and Merriton Landing. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, particularly by older residents, so a phrasebook helps outside the city.

There is one hospital and several clinics on the island. The hospital has many multilingual employees. Travel insurance is recommended.

Violent crime is very rare. As tourism has grown there are more reports of pickpocketing and other petty crime, so keep valuables close in busy areas such as the harbor and Merriton Landing.

Taniti has a large number of national holidays, and many attractions and restaurants close on them. Check the holiday dates for your travel week before you plan a day out, or ask the visitor center.

Most visitors stay between two and seven nights. Five or six nights is enough to see the city, the rainforest, and the volcano without rushing.
