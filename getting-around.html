Getting around

Taniti City is flat and very walkable, and the area around Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot. For everything else, here are your options.

Arriving on the island

By air

Almost all visitors arrive by air. Taniti is served by a small airport that accommodates small jets and propeller planes. The airport is being expanded so that larger jets can land within the next few years.

By cruise ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week. The dock is a short walk from Taniti City, so a day ashore does not require a car.

From the airport to your hotel

Four ways to make the trip, whether or not you rent a car.

Hotel shuttle

The resort and several hotels meet arriving flights. Ask when you book, or request an airport shuttle with your booking.

Taxi

Taxis are available in Taniti City and meet flights at the airport. No advance booking is needed.

Public bus

Public buses serve Taniti City and run from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. every day, so a bus into the city is available for almost every arriving flight.

Rental car

Cars are rented from a local agency near the airport. A car is useful for the volcano and the interior, but it is not needed to stay in the city.

Travelling around the island

Buses

Public buses run in Taniti City from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island, including the routes toward the rainforest and the volcano.

Taxis and rental cars

Taxis operate in Taniti City. Rental cars come from the local agency near the airport, and are the most common choice for visitors who plan to cross the island.

Bicycles

Bikes and helmets are rented from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable, and the area around Merriton Landing, where many visitors stay, is easy to explore on foot.
