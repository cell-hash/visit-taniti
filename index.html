Good to know before you fly

Currency

The U.S. dollar. Euros and yen are widely accepted, and banks exchange currency.

Language

English is spoken by many younger Tanitians in Taniti City and Merriton Landing.

Drinking age

18. Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

National holidays

Taniti has many national holidays, and attractions may close. Check before you plan a day out.

A small island, and that is the point

Taniti is under 500 square miles and home to about 20,000 people. In one trip you can stand on a black rock beach in the morning, walk a rainforest trail after lunch, and eat fish that was landed that day. Fishing and farming built this island long before tourism did, and you will still see both from the road.

A six-day family trip, as an example

  1. Days 1 and 2 — Taniti City. Settle into your hotel, walk the white sand beaches around Yellow Leaf Bay, and visit the local history museum. See the activities
  2. Day 3 — The volcano. Take the guided half-day hike to Taniti's active volcano. Book the volcano hike
  3. Day 4 — The rainforest. Zip-line through the canopy, then take the guided rainforest hike. Compare both tours
  4. Day 5 — On the water. Snorkel the bay or take a chartered fishing tour. See water activities
  5. Day 6 — Merriton Landing. Art galleries and the arcade in the afternoon, dinner and the microbrewery in the evening. Find a restaurant
