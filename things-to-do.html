Things to do

Most visitors come for the beaches, the rainforest, and the volcano. There is a good deal more than that.

A nine-hole golf course is expected to open next year.

Guided volcano hike

Half day · $85 per adult, $45 per child Family friendly

A guided hike on Taniti's small active volcano in the mountainous interior. Groups leave Taniti City at 7:00 a.m. and return by early afternoon. Suitable for children aged eight and older.

Book this activity

Yellow Leaf Bay beaches

Any time · Free Family friendly

White sand beaches encircle the bay next to Taniti City, and rocky beaches sit further along the coast. No booking needed.

Book this activity

Rainforest zip-line tour

2 hours · $75 per person Family friendly

A canopy zip-line course through the lush tropical rainforest. Minimum age ten.

Book this activity

Guided rainforest hike

Half day · $45 per person Family friendly

A walking tour of the rainforest with a Tanitian guide, at an easy pace for families.

Book this activity

Snorkeling trip

3 hours · $60 per person Family friendly

A boat trip to the reef inside Yellow Leaf Bay. Equipment is included and beginners are welcome.

Book this activity

Chartered fishing tour

Half day · $180 per boat (up to 6 people)

Local crews run charters from the harbor. Taniti's fishing fleet has worked these waters for generations.

Book this activity

Taniti History Museum

1 to 2 hours · $8 per adult, children free Family friendly

The island's history, and the native architecture the city is known for. Closed on national holidays.

Book this activity

Island boat or bus tour

Half day · $55 per person Family friendly

See the island beyond the city, including the coastline and the interior, without driving yourself.

Book this activity

Helicopter island flight

30 minutes · $220 per person

An aerial circuit of the volcano, the rainforest, and the harbor.

Book this activity

Merriton Landing after dark

Evenings · Varies

The rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay holds several pubs including a microbrewery, a new dance club, a cinema, an arcade, art galleries, and bowling. Most visitors walk between them.
